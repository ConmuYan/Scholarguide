# Track B World Model review status

- This is a working research snapshot, not a claim of exhaustive independent verification.
- The frozen corpus was completed under a single-reviewer policy; independent cross-review was explicitly waived. A deterministic 25% sample is retained for future sign-off.
- Nineteen records without a resolved OpenAlex citation count were assigned zero under the documented expedited freeze policy.
- GitHub evidence contains 9 verified official repositories and 119 verified `none_available` outcomes. Negative repository outcomes should receive future spot checks before a formal benchmark release.
- Fifty-four records use the documented conservative 0.40 institution floor where stored affiliation evidence did not map to the rubric.
- ScholarGuide retrieval runs and nDCG metrics are downstream evaluation artifacts and are intentionally not part of this corpus submission.
