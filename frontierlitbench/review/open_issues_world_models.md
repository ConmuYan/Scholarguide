# Track B World Model review status

- This is a working research snapshot, not a claim of exhaustive independent verification.
- The corpus uses a primary single-reviewer policy; independent cross-review was explicitly waived. A deterministic 25% sample is retained for future sign-off.
- Thirteen of the 128 Gold records have citation status `verified_absent_counted_zero_single_reviewer_freeze` and retain zero without an observed OpenAlex citation count. Missing counts are not verified zero; these records and their labels need re-adjudication before final release. The earlier count of nineteen was stale.
- The historical 2026-11-11 repository snapshot records 65 official repositories and 63 `none_available` outcomes. Subsequent review found two official repositories incorrectly marked `none_available`: VJEPA (`candidate:wm:v2:149`) and PERSIST (`candidate:wm:v2:134`). See `trackb_repository_additional_evidence_20261111.json`. Repository scores and ranks must be recomputed from a consistent new snapshot.
- Fifty-four records use the documented conservative 0.40 institution floor where stored affiliation evidence did not map to the rubric.
- An unmapped affiliation does not establish that the institution belongs in the 0.40 group. Confirm publication-time affiliation and, where applicable, the industrial-lab mapping before final labels are produced.
- Publication-time first-author affiliation evidence has been collected for 13 of those 54 records in `trackb_affiliation_evidence_20261111.json`; this is evidence for review, not a recalculated score. An affiliation still needs an applicable rubric mapping.
- Lune retrieval on 2026-11-11 found some papers whose OpenAlex counts are missing, including the 2024 hierarchical world model paper with 24 citations in Lune. This demonstrates why missing cannot be assumed to mean zero. Lune counts have a different source and observation time and must not be mixed into the historical OpenAlex percentile calculation.
- Run `python frontierlitbench/scripts/track_b_review_queue.py` to list affected records and supplementary evidence. Once citation counts and institutional mappings are resolved, refresh repository observations together, recompute all year percentiles, ranks and tiers, and publish a new corpus version. Until then the historical tiers and downstream scores are provisional.
- ScholarGuide retrieval runs and nDCG metrics are downstream evaluation artifacts and are intentionally not part of this corpus submission.
